Respectful participation

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Use your account responsibly. Do not impersonate another person, share another person's private information without permission, or use the community to target or intimidate anyone.

Reporting and blocking

If you encounter objectionable content, use Report post in the post menu or the Report control on a comment. You can also report a member in the member directory. Include a reason so moderators can review the concern.

Use Block author or the member blocking control to hide an abusive member's content from your community view. For help or concerns that need support, contact hello@dirtyturf.com.

Dirty Turf moderators review reports and can remove or hide objectionable content and suspend community access for abusive users. Agreeing to these rules does not permit prohibited content, and reporting or blocking does not replace moderator review.

Only share material you are allowed to share. Respect other members' content, training materials, and personal information.
