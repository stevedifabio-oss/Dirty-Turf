Signing in

Use the same email attached to your Academy membership. The app sends a one-time Magic Link and does not require a password. Open the newest link on the device where you want to sign in. Expired or already-used links cannot be reused.

If the email does not arrive, check spam, wait a minute before requesting another link, and confirm that the address matches your membership.

Course access

A successful sign-in does not create or change a paid membership. If you can sign in but see an access message, contact support so the existing Academy grant can be reconciled. Include the account email and the course or lesson title.

Measurements

Map tracing works on desktop and mobile. Live point-to-point camera measurement requires a supported iPhone with ARKit or Android phone with ARCore. A normal browser camera cannot estimate real-world distance accurately, so the web app offers map and manual measurement instead.

For an unexpected result, include the measurement method, expected dimensions, device model, operating-system version, and a screenshot. Do not include a customer address unless support needs it to reproduce the issue.

Billing

Billing is not available in the iPhone or Android apps. Contact hello@dirtyturf.com for membership or billing help.

Before contacting support

  1. Update the browser or app and retry on a stable connection.
  2. Confirm the sign-in email and request a fresh Magic Link.
  3. Record the device, browser or app version, exact screen, and what happened.
  4. Email hello@dirtyturf.com. Never send passwords, Magic Links, API keys, or card details.
